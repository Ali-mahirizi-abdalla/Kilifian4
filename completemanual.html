POLICY 1: CODE OF CONDUCT

1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical and professional standards expected of all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, and partners. It promotes integrity, accountability, and respect in all KISI activities.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, consultants, contractors, and partner organisations.

3. Core Principles

  • Integrity: Act honestly, transparently, and ethically at all times.
  • Accountability: Take responsibility for actions and decisions.
  • Respect: Treat all persons with dignity regardless of gender, age, disability, ethnicity, or religion.
  • Confidentiality: Protect sensitive information entrusted to KISI.
  • Non-Discrimination: Reject all forms of discrimination, harassment, and exploitation.
  • Compliance: Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and KISI policies.
  • Conflict of Interest: Declare and avoid personal interests that conflict with KISI's mission.

4. Expected Behaviour

DO DO NOT
  • Act with honesty and fairness.
  • Use KISI resources responsibly.
  • Report misconduct promptly.
  • Maintain professional boundaries.
  • Respect cultural diversity.
  • Accept bribes, kickbacks, or gifts.
  • Misuse KISI funds, assets, or information.
  • Engage in harassment or discrimination.
  • Retaliate against whistleblowers.
  • Represent KISI without authorisation.

5. Conflicts of Interest

All persons must declare any actual or potential conflict of interest to the Management Committee and recuse themselves from related decisions.

6. Use of Resources

KISI resources (funds, equipment, vehicles, information) must be used solely for KISI's objectives and in accordance with KISI's Financial Management Policy.

7. Breach and Sanctions

Level Example Sanction
Minor Lateness, rudeness Verbal warning; KES 100 fine
Moderate Repeated absence, misuse of petty funds Written warning; suspension
Serious Fraud, harassment, abuse Expulsion; referral to authorities

8. Reporting

Report breaches to the Secretary or Chairperson. Anonymous reports may be submitted via the KISI Whistleblowing Policy.

9. Review

This Code shall be reviewed every two years or as needed.

POLICY 2: CHILD PROTECTION POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to protecting all children (under 18 years) involved in its activities from harm, abuse, exploitation, and neglect. This policy ensures a safe environment for children in all KISI programmes.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, partners, contractors, and visitors who come into contact with children through KISI activities.

3. Guiding Principles

  • Best Interests of the Child: The child's safety and well-being come first.
  • Zero Tolerance: No form of child abuse is acceptable.
  • Participation: Children have a right to be heard on matters affecting them.
  • Non-Discrimination: All children are protected equally.
  • Confidentiality: Child protection information is handled sensitively.

4. Forms of Child Abuse

Type Description
Physical Non-accidental injury to a child
Emotional Persistent ill-treatment affecting emotional development
Sexual Any sexual act or exploitation involving a child
Neglect Failure to meet basic needs (food, shelter, health, education)
Exploitation Child labour, trafficking, or any form of economic exploitation

5. Code of Conduct for Working with Children

  • Always work in pairs or visible areas with children.
  • Never spend time alone with a child in a secluded place.
  • Never use physical punishment or threatening language.
  • Never develop inappropriate relationships with children.
  • Never take photographs or videos without parental/guardian consent.
  • Never give gifts or money without prior approval.
  • Report any concern immediately.

6. Safe Recruitment

All persons working with children must provide: National ID or passport, Police clearance certificate, two references, signed safeguarding declaration, completion of child protection training.

7. Reporting and Response

Step Action Responsible
1 Ensure immediate safety of the child Any person
2 Report to Safeguarding Focal Point within 24 hours Any person
3 Document the concern Safeguarding Focal Point
4 Report to authorities (Children's Services, Police) Safeguarding Focal Point
5 Support the child (counselling, referral) Safeguarding Focal Point
6 Investigate and take disciplinary action Management Committee

POLICY 3: ANTI-FRAUD AND ANTI-CORRUPTION POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and accountability. This policy establishes KISI's zero-tolerance stance against fraud, corruption, bribery, and financial malpractice.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, consultants, contractors, partners, suppliers, and beneficiaries.

3. Definitions

Term Definition
Fraud Intentional deception for personal or organisational gain
Corruption Abuse of entrusted power for private gain
Bribery Offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting something of value to influence action
Embezzlement Misappropriation of funds or assets entrusted to one's care
Conflict of Interest Personal interest that conflicts with KISI's interest
Kickback Illegal payment for favourable treatment

4. Prohibited Acts

  • Soliciting, accepting, or offering bribes or kickbacks.
  • Falsifying financial records, receipts, or reports.
  • Misappropriating KISI funds, assets, or property.
  • Processing fraudulent payments or invoices.
  • Using KISI resources for personal benefit.
  • Concealing or failing to declare conflicts of interest.
  • Colluding with suppliers or partners to defraud KISI.
  • Nepotism or favouritism in recruitment or procurement.

5. Preventive Measures

Area Control
Financial Management Dual signatories; monthly reconciliations; annual audits
Procurement Competitive bidding; three quotations; procurement committee
Cash Handling Receipts for all payments; cash book; surprise cash counts
Banking Two signatories (Treasurer mandatory); no blank cheques
Asset Management Asset register; regular physical verification
Human Resources Reference checks; conflict-of-interest declarations
Reporting Whistleblowing channels; quarterly financial reports

6. Reporting Suspected Fraud or Corruption

  1. Report immediately to the Treasurer, Chairperson, or via the Whistleblowing Policy.
  2. Provide a written statement with details (date, persons, amounts, evidence).
  3. Preserve all relevant documents and evidence.
  4. Cooperate fully with investigations.

7. Investigation Procedure

Step Action Responsible
1 Receive and document report Treasurer / Chairperson
2 Constitute investigation committee (3 members) Management Committee
3 Conduct investigation (max 30 days) Investigation Committee
4 Submit findings and recommendations Investigation Committee
5 Take disciplinary or legal action Management Committee
6 Report to authorities where criminal Chairperson

8. Sanctions

  • Recovery of misappropriated funds or assets.
  • Written warning, suspension, or expulsion.
  • Termination of contracts with partners or suppliers.
  • Referral to Kenya Police and/or Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission (EACC).
  • Legal action for criminal or civil recovery.

9. Protection of Reporters

KISI will protect persons who report suspected fraud or corruption in good faith from retaliation, victimisation, or discrimination.

10. Legal Framework

  • Anti-Corruption and Economic Crimes Act 2003
  • Bribery Act 2016
  • Public Officer Ethics Act 2003
  • Penal Code (Cap 63)
  • Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act 2015

11. Review

This policy shall be reviewed every two years.

POLICY 4: WHISTLEBLOWING POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to transparency and accountability. This policy encourages and protects individuals who report suspected wrongdoing, malpractice, or unethical conduct within KISI or its partners.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, consultants, contractors, partners, and beneficiaries who wish to report wrongdoing.

3. What Can Be Reported

  • Fraud, corruption, or bribery
  • Financial mismanagement or embezzlement
  • Child abuse or safeguarding violations
  • Sexual harassment or discrimination
  • Abuse of power or authority
  • Breach of KISI policies or laws
  • Environmental harm or violations
  • Health and safety violations
  • Other serious misconduct

4. Reporting Channels

Channel Contact Suitable For

Treasurer 0720468616 Financial concerns
Secretary 0790592884 Governance concerns
Anonymous Email [To be established] Sensitive concerns
External — EACC 1517 (toll-free) Criminal matters

5. How to Report

  1. Provide your name (unless anonymous).
  2. Describe the wrongdoing clearly (who, what, when, where, how).
  3. Provide evidence if available.
  4. State if you have reported elsewhere.
  5. Submit via any channel above.

6. Anonymous Reporting

KISI accepts anonymous reports. However, anonymous reports may be harder to investigate. KISI will investigate all reports to the extent possible.

7. Protection of Whistleblowers

  • Confidentiality: Identity kept confidential to the extent possible.
  • Non-Retaliation: No retaliation, victimisation, or discrimination.
  • Support: Counselling and support available if needed.
  • Legal Protection: Protected under the Kenya Whistleblower Protection framework.

8. Investigation Procedure

2 Preliminary assessment 14 days
3 Full investigation 30–90 days
4 Report findings Within 7 days of completion
5 Action and feedback Within 30 days of findings

9. False Reports

Knowingly making false or malicious reports is a breach of this policy and may lead to disciplinary action.

10. Record Keeping

All reports and investigations will be documented and stored securely for a minimum of seven years.

11. Review

POLICY 5: DATA PROTECTION POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI collects, processes, and stores personal data of members, beneficiaries, staff, volunteers, and partners. This policy ensures compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and protects the privacy of data subjects.

2. Scope

Applies to all personal data collected, stored, or processed by KISI in any format (paper, electronic, or verbal).

3. Definitions

Term Definition
Personal Data Any information relating to an identified or identifiable person
Data Subject The individual to whom personal data relates
Data Controller KISI (determines purpose and means of processing)
Data Processor Any party processing data on behalf of KISI
Sensitive Data Health, biometric, religious, political, or sexual orientation data

4. Data Protection Principles

  1. Lawfulness: Process data lawfully and fairly.
  2. Purpose Limitation: Collect data for specific, legitimate purposes.
  3. Data Minimisation: Collect only what is necessary.
  4. Accuracy: Keep data accurate and up to date.
  5. Storage Limitation: Retain only as long as necessary.
  6. Integrity and Confidentiality: Protect data with appropriate security.
  7. Accountability: Be able to demonstrate compliance.

5. Rights of Data Subjects

  • Right to be informed
  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making

6. Data Collection and Consent

  • KISI will obtain explicit consent before collecting personal data.
  • Consent will be in writing or recorded verbally with a witness.
  • Data subjects may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Children's data requires parental/guardian consent.
  • Data will be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes only.

7. Data Security

Measure Description
Physical Locked cabinets; restricted access to offices
Electronic Password-protected devices; encryption; firewalls
Access Control Role-based access; authorisation required
Backup Regular backups; secure off-site storage
Training Staff and volunteers trained on data protection
Incident Response Procedure for reporting and managing data breaches

8. Data Sharing

  • Data will only be shared with consent or legal obligation.
  • Data processors must sign a data processing agreement.
  • Cross-border transfers will comply with the Data Protection Act.
  • KISI will not sell personal data.

9. Data Retention

Type of Data Retention Period
Membership records 7 years after membership ends
Financial records 7 years
Beneficiary data 5 years after project ends
Safeguarding records Permanent or as required by law
Project M&E data 5 years

10. Data Breach Response

  1. Contain the breach immediately.
  2. Assess the risk to data subjects.
  3. Notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) within 72 hours if required.
  4. Notify affected data subjects if high risk.
  5. Document the breach and lessons learned.

11. Data Protection Officer

KISI shall designate a Data Protection Focal Point responsible for compliance with this policy. Contact: [Name], [Phone], [Email].

12. Legal Framework

  • Kenya Data Protection Act 2019
  • Constitution of Kenya 2010 (Article 31 — Right to Privacy)
  • Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act 2018
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable

13. Review

This policy shall be reviewed every two years.

POLICY 6: GENDER EQUALITY POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to promoting gender equality, women's empowerment, and the inclusion of all genders in its governance, programmes, and operations. This policy ensures that KISI's work advances gender equity and challenges harmful social norms.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, partners, contractors, and beneficiaries.

3. Guiding Principles

  • Equality: Equal rights, responsibilities, and opportunities for all genders.
  • Non-Discrimination: Zero tolerance for gender-based discrimination.
  • Empowerment: Actively promote women's leadership and economic empowerment.
  • Inclusion: Recognise and include women, men, girls, boys, and gender minorities.
  • Intersectionality: Recognise how gender intersects with age, disability, ethnicity, and class.
  • Do No Harm: Ensure interventions do not reinforce gender inequalities.

4. Key Commitments

Governance At least 50% women in management committee and leadership roles
Employment Equal pay for equal work; family-friendly policies; maternity and paternity leave
Programmes Gender analysis in project design; sex-disaggregated data; gender-sensitive indicators

GBV Prevention Prevention, referral pathways, and support for survivors

  • Support women's economic empowerment through entrepreneurship.

  • Challenge harmful cultural practices (e.g., early marriage, FGM).

POLICY 7: HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to providing a safe and healthy environment for all staff, volunteers, members, beneficiaries, and visitors. This policy outlines KISI's commitment to preventing accidents, injuries, and occupational illnesses.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, consultants, contractors, partners, and beneficiaries at KISI premises, project sites, and activities.

3. Guiding Principles

  • Duty of Care: KISI has a duty to protect the health and safety of all persons involved in its activities.
  • Prevention: Proactive measures to prevent accidents and illnesses.
  • Participation: All persons have a role in maintaining safety.
  • Compliance: Comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA) 2007.
  • Continuous Improvement: Regularly review and improve safety practices.

4. Responsibilities

Role Responsibilities
Management Committee Approve policy; allocate resources; review incidents
Chairperson Overall accountability for health and safety
Health and Safety Officer Coordinate safety programmes; conduct inspections; report incidents
Supervisors Enforce safety rules; train workers; respond to incidents
All Staff and Volunteers Follow safety rules; report hazards; use protective equipment

5. General Safety Rules

  • Follow all safety instructions and signage.
  • Use personal protective equipment (PPE) where provided.
  • Report hazards, accidents, and near-misses immediately.
  • Keep work areas clean and tidy.
  • Do not operate equipment without training.
  • Do not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Know emergency procedures (fire, first aid, evacuation).

6. Specific Hazards and Controls

Activity Hazard Control
Generator Construction Cuts, electric shock Gloves, eye protection, trained supervision
Neem Processing Dust, allergic reactions Masks, ventilation, gloves
BSF Farming Biological hazards, odours Gloves, masks, hygiene protocols
Water Kiosk Operations Slips, trips, contamination Non-slip surfaces, clean water, hygiene training
Water Plant Operations Electrical, chemical (RO), confined spaces PPE, lockout/tagout, confined space training
Community Barazas Crowd control, COVID-19 Ventilation, masks, hygiene, crowd management
Construction Falls, injuries Harnesses, helmets, safe scaffolding

7. First Aid

  • First aid kits available at all project sites.
  • Trained first aiders identified.
  • Emergency contact numbers displayed.
  • Referral to nearest health facility.

8. Emergency Procedures

Emergency Action
Fire Raise alarm; evacuate; call 999/112
Medical Administer first aid; call ambulance; notify next of kin
Accident Secure scene; administer first aid; report
Natural Disaster Follow evacuation plan; assemble at designated point
Security Follow security protocol; call police

9. Incident Reporting

  1. Report all incidents, injuries, and near-misses.
  2. Complete an Incident Report Form within 24 hours.
  3. Submit to the Health and Safety Officer.
  4. Investigate to prevent recurrence.
  5. Report to authorities as required by law.

10. Training

  • Induction training on health and safety for all new staff and volunteers.
  • Annual refresher training.
  • Specialised training for high-risk activities.
  • First aid training for designated personnel.

11. Legal Framework

  • Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA) 2007
  • Work Injury Benefits Act (WIBA) 2007

POLICY 8: FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. Purpose

KISI is committed to prudent financial management, transparency, and accountability. This policy establishes the systems and procedures for managing KISI's financial resources in accordance with Kenyan law and donor requirements.

2. Scope

Applies to all KISI funds, assets, and financial transactions, including grants, donations, membership fees, and social enterprise revenue.

3. Guiding Principles

  • Accountability: All funds are accounted for and used for intended purposes.
  • Transparency: Financial information is accessible to members and donors.
  • Integrity: Financial transactions are honest and ethical.
  • Compliance: Comply with all applicable laws and donor requirements.
  • Value for Money: Achieve best value in all expenditures.
  • Segregation of Duties: No single person controls all aspects of a transaction.

4. Roles and Responsibilities

Role Responsibilities
Management Committee Approve budgets; review financial reports; oversight
Treasurer Day-to-day financial management; banking; record-keeping; reporting
Chairperson Signatory; oversight; authorisation
Secretary Signatory; record-keeping (non-financial)
Internal Audit Committee Quarterly internal audits; report to Management Committee
External Auditor Annual external audit; report to AGM

5. Banking Arrangements

  • KISI shall maintain a bank account at a reputable financial institution.
  • Signatories: Chairperson, Treasurer (mandatory), Secretary.
  • Two signatories required for all withdrawals.
  • No blank cheques.
  • Bank reconciliations monthly.
  • Bank statements reviewed by Management Committee quarterly.

6. Budgeting

  • Annual budget prepared by Treasurer and approved by Management Committee.
  • Project budgets prepared for each project and approved before implementation.
  • Budget vs actual analysis conducted monthly.
  • Budget revisions require Management Committee approval.

7. Expenditure Procedures

Step Action Responsible
1 Request for expenditure (requisition) Project lead
2 Approval Treasurer / Chairperson
3 Procurement (where applicable) Procurement committee
4 Payment processing Treasurer
5 Receipt / voucher Payee
6 Recording Treasurer
7 Filing Treasurer

8. Procurement

  • Competitive bidding for purchases above KES 20,000.
  • Three quotations required for purchases KES 5,000 – 20,000.
  • Direct purchase allowed for items below KES 5,000.
  • Procurement committee (3 members) for large purchases.
  • Conflict of interest declared and recused.
  • All purchases supported by receipts and delivery notes.

9. Cash Management

  • Petty cash float of KES 10,000 maximum.
  • Petty cash book maintained.
  • Receipts for all petty cash payments.
  • Surprise cash counts by Internal Audit Committee.
  • Cash deposited in bank within 7 days.

10. Financial Records

KISI shall maintain: cash book, bank reconciliation statements, payment vouchers, receipt books, general ledger, asset register, payroll records, budget and financial reports, and audit reports. Records shall be retained for a minimum of seven years.

11. Financial Reporting

Report Frequency Audience
Monthly Financial Report Monthly Management Committee
Quarterly Financial Report Quarterly Management Committee, Donors
Annual Financial Report Annually AGM, Donors
Project Financial Report As required Donors
Audited Accounts Annually AGM, Donors, Authorities

12. Auditing

  • Internal Audit: Conducted quarterly by a 3-member committee (non-signatories).
  • External Audit: Conducted annually by an independent auditor.
  • Donor Audit: Conducted as required by donor agreements.
  • Audit findings presented to AGM and Management Committee.
  • Action plan for audit recommendations.

13. Asset Management

  • Asset register maintained for all assets above KES 5,000.
  • Assets tagged and verified annually.
  • Disposal of assets requires Management Committee approval.
  • Assets insured where appropriate.

14. Fraud Prevention

Refer to KISI Anti-Fraud and Anti-Corruption Policy. Key controls include: segregation of duties, dual signatories, regular reconciliations, internal audits, whistleblowing channels, and conflict of interest declarations.

This policy shall be reviewed every two years.

POLICY 9: SAFEGUARDING POLICY

1. Introduction and Purpose

Kilifian4 Integrity and Sustainability Initiative (KISI) is a community-based organisation registered in Kilifi County, Kenya. KISI is committed to promoting sustainable livelihoods, environmental conservation, and community health through integrity-driven, locally appropriate innovations.

KISI recognises that its work brings it into contact with vulnerable individuals, including women, girls, boys, young mothers, persons with disabilities, the elderly, and marginalised groups. This Safeguarding Policy sets out KISI's commitment to protecting these individuals from harm, abuse, exploitation, and neglect.

The purpose of this policy is to:

  1. Establish clear safeguarding standards for all KISI staff, volunteers, partners, and associates.
  2. Prevent harm to children, young people, and vulnerable adults involved in KISI's activities.
  3. Provide clear procedures for reporting, responding to, and managing safeguarding concerns.
  4. Ensure compliance with Kenyan law and international safeguarding standards, including the Commonwealth Foundation's requirements.
  5. Promote a culture of safety, dignity, and respect throughout the organisation.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, partner organisations, consultants, contractors, and visitors. It covers all KISI activities, including community mobilisation, training programmes, water kiosk operations, neem processing, BSF farming, generator construction, health education, M&E, and digital library programmes.

3. Definitions

Term Definition
Child Any person under the age of 18 years
Vulnerable Adult Any person aged 18 or over who is unable to protect themselves from harm or exploitation
Safeguarding Measures taken to protect children and vulnerable adults from harm
Harm Physical, emotional, sexual, or psychological injury or damage
Abuse Physical, emotional, sexual, or financial mistreatment
Exploitation Taking advantage of a person for personal gain
Neglect Failure to provide necessary care, supervision, or protection
Safeguarding Concern Any suspicion, allegation, or disclosure of harm
Safeguarding Focal Point Designated KISI officer responsible for safeguarding
Mandatory Reporting Legal obligation to report certain types of harm to authorities

4. Policy Statement

KISI is committed to: Zero Tolerance; Best Interests; Safe Recruitment; Awareness and Training; Reporting and Response; Confidentiality; Non-Retaliation; Accountability; Legal Compliance; and Continuous Improvement.

5. Code of Conduct

DO: Treat all individuals with dignity, respect, and fairness; create a safe and inclusive environment; be aware of power imbalances; report any safeguarding concerns immediately; follow KISI's safeguarding procedures; cooperate fully with investigations; maintain appropriate professional boundaries; be vigilant and proactive in protecting vulnerable persons.

DO NOT: Engage in any form of abuse; use inappropriate language or behaviour; develop inappropriate relationships; give gifts or money without approval; take photographs or videos without consent; share personal information without authorisation; spend time alone with a child in a secluded place; engage in exploitative or harmful activities; retaliate against anyone who reports a concern.

Identity Verification; Background Checks (Police clearance); Reference Checks (two); Qualification Verification; Safeguarding Declaration; Probation Period.

ANNEX B: SAFEGUARDING CODE OF CONDUCT

1. Purpose

This Safeguarding Code of Conduct sets out the behaviour expected of all KISI office bearers, members, staff, volunteers, partners, and visitors when working with children and vulnerable adults.

2. Scope

Applies to all persons engaged in KISI activities, whether paid or unpaid, formal or informal.

3. Our Commitments

I, as a person working with or on behalf of KISI, commit to the standards below.

4. DO — Expected Behaviour

  • Treat all children and vulnerable adults with dignity and respect.
  • Create a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment.
  • Be aware of power imbalances and never exploit them.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.
  • Work in pairs or visible areas whenever with children.
  • Report any safeguarding concern immediately.
  • Comply with KISI's Safeguarding Policy and related procedures.
  • Cooperate fully with any safeguarding investigation.
  • Attend safeguarding training and refresher sessions.
  • Respect cultural diversity and local norms.
  • Use appropriate, non-discriminatory language.
  • Obtain consent before taking photographs or videos.

5. DO NOT — Prohibited Behaviour

  • Engage in physical, emotional, sexual, or financial abuse.
  • Develop inappropriate relationships with children or vulnerable adults.
  • Spend time alone with a child in a secluded place.
  • Give gifts, money, or favours without prior approval.
  • Take photographs or videos without consent.
  • Share personal information without authorisation.
  • Use inappropriate language, gestures, or behaviour.
  • Administer physical punishment or threaten harm.
  • Discriminate based on gender, age, disability, ethnicity, religion, or sexual orientation.
  • Retaliate against anyone who reports a safeguarding concern.
  • Work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Engage in any activity that could be perceived as exploitative or harmful.

6. Special Provisions for Working with Children

  • Always have a second adult present or within sight.
  • Use open, public spaces for activities.
  • Respect children's privacy and dignity.
  • Encourage children to speak up about any concerns.
  • Never use children for personal, political, or financial gain.
  • Report any suspicion of abuse or neglect immediately.

7. Special Provisions for Working with Vulnerable Adults

  • Respect autonomy and informed consent.
  • Protect against financial exploitation.
  • Ensure privacy and confidentiality.
  • Adapt communication to their needs.
  • Report any concerns to the Safeguarding Focal Point.

8. Reporting Obligations

I understand that I must report any safeguarding concern immediately; reporting can be done to the Safeguarding Focal Point or any KISI office bearer; anonymous reporting is accepted; and failure to report is itself a breach of this Code.

9. Consequences of Breach

Level Example Action
Minor Inappropriate language, lateness Verbal warning; further training
Moderate Breach of boundaries, unauthorised photos Written warning; suspension
Serious Abuse, exploitation, failure to report abuse Immediate dismissal; referral to authorities

ANNEX D: SAFEGUARDING REFERRAL PATHWAYS

1. Purpose

This annex provides clear referral pathways for handling safeguarding concerns, ensuring that affected persons receive timely, appropriate, and confidential support.

2. Internal Referral Pathway

Step 1: Any person who witnesses or receives a disclosure of harm reports immediately to the Safeguarding Focal Point or any KISI office bearer.
Step 2: Safeguarding Focal Point ensures immediate safety of the affected person and documents the concern within 24 hours.
Step 3: Safeguarding Focal Point conducts a preliminary risk assessment within 48 hours.
Step 4: If risk is high, Safeguarding Focal Point reports to authorities (Police, Children's Services, or Health Facility) immediately.
Step 5: Safeguarding Focal Point informs Management Committee and coordinates further response.
Step 6: KISI provides support to the affected person (counselling, medical, legal) and monitors the situation.
Step 7: Case is documented, closed, and lessons learned are integrated into KISI practice.
