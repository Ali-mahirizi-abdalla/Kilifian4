Connecting Ethical Integrity & Ecological Sustainability: The KISI Journey

KISI Chairperson

Initiative Leader
Jul 22, 2026 • 12 Comments

At the heart of the Kilifian4 Integrity & Sustainability Initiative (KISI) is the core goal to champion the inseparable connection between ethical integrity and ecological sustainability.

Founded as a non-political, non-denominational membership association under Article 36 of the Constitution of Kenya, KISI coordinates members from Pwani University and structured affiliates. We target community-wide socio-economic empowerment and environmental stewardship through direct, actionable projects.

Our Key Areas of Activity

KISI focuses on sustainable, practical solutions that address immediate community needs while keeping eye on future generations:

  • Smart (LED) Agriculture & Crop Feasibility Lab: Soil assessments and optimized yields.
  • Digital Libraries & Literacy: Providing resources and IT tools for local students.
  • Women & Child Empowerment: Mentoring young mothers and youth support.
  • Climate Resilience & E-Waste: Collecting and recycling electronic waste responsibly.
  • Green Energy & Water: Installing solar clinics and community water tanks.
  • Wellness & Care: Supporting mental health and ageing community wellness.

KISI Core Values

  • Affluence: Wealth, well-being and quality of life
  • Inclusivity & Respect: Collaborate with diversity
  • Resilience & Trust: Management of resources

Governance, Membership & Cohesion

To maintain order and high standards of trust, KISI implements clear rights and etiquette rules. Members hold voting rights and are required to respect community confidentiality, attend meetings, and actively participate. With simple, fair systems including lateness fines and mediation-based dispute resolution, KISI provides a robust model for local community association.

"Keep It Sustainable and Sound — this is our blueprint for local empowerment, showing that clean governance and environmental duty go hand-in-hand."

KISI Executive Board Representative
