About KISI

Kilifian4 Integrity & Sustainability Initiative (KISI) is a non-political, non-denominational membership association registered in Kenya.

Our Goal

Connecting Ethical Integrity & Ecological Sustainability

We champion the inseparable connection between ethical integrity and ecological sustainability, accelerating the global transition toward societies that are transparently governed, socially just, and environmentally regenerative.

KISI aims to empower and transform society through the power of collaboration and association. We are non-political and non-denominational, focused entirely on social support and economic empowerment of our communities.

Established under the Constitution of Kenya, Article 36, KISI brings together Kenyan citizens in Pwani University and structured affiliates to cooperate in development, lifelong learning, and sustainable agriculture.

Our Purpose

To empower and transform society through the power of collaboration and association, focusing on development projects.

Values-driven

Completely non-political and non-denominational, prioritizing social support, community wellness, and economic progress.

Core Values

Rooted in Affluence (wealth & well-being), Inclusivity, Resilience, Respect, and Trust (responsible resource management).
